Anatomy

  • Label above the control for entry fields; label left for preference rows (a toggle you flip, not a value you type).
  • Help sits below in --faint; on error it is replaced by the message in --neg, never stacked.
  • Required is a mint *; optional is said in words. Don't mark both.
  • Validate on blur, not per keystroke — the slug erred only after the field was left.
  • Focus is a real state: :focus on text fields, :focus-visible on buttons & controls.
  • One accent. Mint marks focus, the checked mark, and "on" — nothing decorative.
