Composed Recipe · Inbox / messaging · one skin, five compositions

Same skin. Five ways to read a message.

One conversation — the renewals thread between the team and Northwind — composed five ways. Every frame wears the same neutral skin: off-white page, white surfaces with hairline borders, slightly-rounded corners, one restrained blue accent, system sans with tabular figures. What changes is purely composition and interaction. The skin is the paint, the composition is the skeleton.

Composition 1
Three-pane email

PULLS — left folder/label rail (Inbox, Sent… with unread counts) + middle message list (sender, subject, preview, time, unread dot) + right reading pane with an open message and reply bar.  WHY — the classic email layout: folders scope, the list scans, the pane reads — three densities side by side so you triage and read without navigating.

Composition 2
Two-pane chat / DM

PULLS — left conversation list (avatar + name + last-message preview + time) + right open thread (message bubbles left/right, a day divider) + a composer at the bottom.  WHY — for real-time messaging: bubbles convey turn-taking and direction at a glance, and a persistent composer keeps the conversation moving.

Composition 3
Unified inbox + filters

PULLS — a single full-width list with segmented tabs (All / Unread / Mentions) + row checkboxes that reveal a bulk-action bar (Archive, Mark read, Delete).  WHY — when one queue mixes sources: tabs slice by state and multi-select makes clearing a backlog a batch operation rather than one-by-one.

Composition 4
Focused conversation

PULLS — one centered conversation thread with an inline quick-reply box and thread actions (reply, forward, resolve) in a header.  WHY — for support or single-issue threads: removing list chrome puts the whole exchange in one readable column, with resolve turning a conversation into a closeable task.

Apply
Skins & how to apply them

A skin is the full set of visual tokens — color, type, radius, elevation, and density — applied uniformly. Pick ONE skin per product and apply it across whichever compositions you choose: the composition is the skeleton, the skin is the paint, so any composition above renders coherently in any skin. Every frame on this page uses the Neutral column.
