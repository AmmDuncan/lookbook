Same skin. Five ways to price it.

One set of plans — Starter, Pro, Scale — presented five ways. Every frame wears the same neutral skin: off-white page, white surfaces with hairline borders, slightly-rounded corners, one restrained blue accent, system sans with tabular figures. What changes is purely composition and interaction. The skin is the paint, the composition is the skeleton.

Composition 1
Tier cards

PULLS — three plan cards side by side (Starter / Pro / Scale), the middle highlighted as “Most popular” with a ring + badge; each has price, feature bullets, CTA; a monthly/annual segmented toggle above.  WHY — the conversion default: parallel cards make plans easy to compare, and one anchored “popular” tier guides the choice without removing it.

Composition 2
Comparison table

PULLS — a feature-matrix table (feature rows × plan columns) with check/dash cells + a sticky plan header row + per-column CTA.  WHY — for feature-rich products: a matrix answers “what exactly do I get” precisely, which a card's short bullet list can't, and is the deciding view for considered buyers.

Skins & how to apply them

A skin is the full set of visual tokens — color, type, radius, elevation, and density — applied uniformly. Pick ONE skin per product and apply it across whichever compositions you choose: the composition is the skeleton, the skin is the paint, so any composition above renders coherently in any skin. Every frame on this page uses the Neutral column.
