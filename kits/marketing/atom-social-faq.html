Can I change plans later?
Yes — upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from billing. Changes prorate to the day, and your audit history is never touched.
What happens after the free trial?
You drop to the Starter plan automatically — no card required to start, no surprise charges. Add a card only when you choose to upgrade.
How does reconciliation actually work?
We match every transaction against your connected sources in real time and flag anything that doesn't reconcile, with a full trail of why.
