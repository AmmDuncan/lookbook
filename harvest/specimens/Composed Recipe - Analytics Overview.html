Composed Recipe · Analytics overview · one skin, five compositions

Same skin. Five ways to compose it.

Every frame below wears the same neutral skin — off-white page, white cards with hairline borders, one restrained blue accent, system sans with tabular figures. What changes from one to the next is purely composition and interaction: where the nav lives, how filters are expressed, how the table and chart are arranged, how you drill in. The skin is the paint; the composition is the skeleton.

Composition 1
Classic console

LAYOUT — left sidebar shell + top filter bar · 4-up KPI strip with delta chips · one wide line chart (revenue vs 30-day baseline) + a compact donut · transactions table with status-by-shape badges.  INTERACTION — a date-range segmented control re-scopes every widget at once. The familiar operator default: persistent nav, everything on one screen.

Composition 4
Master-detail

LAYOUT — a list/table on the left paired with a detail panel on the right · the KPI summary and chart belong to whichever row is selected.  INTERACTION — selecting a row on the left updates the entire right panel in place; no navigation, no modal. Best when each record has enough depth to inspect.

Skins & how to apply them

A skin is nothing more than a small set of color + font tokens. Pick ONE skin per product and apply it across whichever compositions you choose — the composition is the skeleton, the skin is the paint. Every composition above is drawn in the Neutral skin; swap the token row and the same skeletons render coherently in any of the others.
