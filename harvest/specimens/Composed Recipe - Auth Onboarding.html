Same skin. Five ways to get in.

The front door — sign in, sign up, verify, and get started — composed five ways. Every frame wears the same neutral skin: off-white page, white surfaces with hairline borders, slightly-rounded corners, one restrained blue accent, system sans. Fields use placeholder labels only. What changes is purely composition and interaction.

Composition 1
Centered card login

PULLS — a centered auth card: email + password fields, primary Sign in, social sign-in buttons, “forgot password” and “create account” links.  WHY — the universal default: a single centered card focuses all attention on one task and reads instantly on any screen size.

Composition 3
Signup + password strength

PULLS — a signup form (name, email, password) with a live password-strength meter + a terms checkbox.  WHY — account creation is where trust starts: real-time strength feedback nudges safer passwords, and an explicit terms check keeps consent unambiguous.

Composition 4
2FA verify code

PULLS — a verification screen: 6 single-digit OTP input boxes + a resend timer + a verify button.  WHY — for a one-time code, discrete boxes show length and progress, auto-advance keeps hands on the keyboard, and a countdown sets expectations for resend.

Composition 5
Welcome / onboarding

PULLS — a post-signup welcome screen with a short setup checklist (3 steps with progress) and a primary “Get started” CTA.  WHY — first impressions drive activation: a warm welcome plus a tiny, finishable checklist turns a new account into momentum instead of a blank slate.

Skins & how to apply them

A skin is the full set of visual tokens — color, type, radius, elevation, and density — applied uniformly. Pick ONE skin per product and apply it across whichever compositions you choose: the composition is the skeleton, the skin is the paint, so any composition above renders coherently in any skin. Every frame on this page uses the Neutral column.
