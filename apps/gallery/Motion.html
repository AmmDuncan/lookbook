∞ — Reduced motion

The fallback contract #reduced

Under @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce), every animation above resolves to its final state instantly. The affordance stays — the motion goes. Test it: toggle reduced motion in your OS, every demo on this page lands in its final frame.

Replace motion with state, never strip the affordance.

A user with vestibular sensitivity still needs to know the modal opened, the toast appeared, the number changed. Render the final frame on the same beat — just without the in-between.
