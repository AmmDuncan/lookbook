Composed Recipe · Card & gallery grid · one skin, five compositions

Same skin. Five ways to grid it.

Every frame wears the same neutral skin — off-white page, white cards with hairline borders, one restrained blue accent, system sans with tabular figures. What changes is purely composition and interaction: how cards are sized and grouped, where filters live, how you browse and select. Loading and empty states are covered; the skin is the paint, the composition is the skeleton.

Composition 1
Gallery grid

PULLS — top toolbar (search + sort dropdown + grid/list view toggle) + responsive 3–4-col card grid (thumbnail + title + meta + tag chip) + numbered pagination · one card hovered reveals a quick-action row.  WHY — the everyday asset/library view: equal cards scan fast, the view toggle respects density preference, and hover actions keep cards calm until you reach for them.

Composition 2
Filter-rail gallery

PULLS — left faceted filter rail (category checkboxes, price & size ranges) + even card grid + removable applied-filter chips above results + “load more” that reveals skeleton cards (the loading state).  WHY — catalogs are narrowed by attributes; the rail and echoed chips keep the active query visible, and skeleton cards make incremental load feel instant rather than empty.

Composition 3
Cover-first / catalog

PULLS — larger cover cards (image + title + price + secondary CTA) + category tabs across the top + a subtle hover lift. No dense data — a browse surface.  WHY — commerce & content catalogs lead with the image; big covers and a single secondary CTA invite browsing, while category tabs scope the shelf without a heavy filter UI.

Composition 4
Compact tiles / dense

PULLS — many small tiles per row (logo/avatar + short label + one stat) + segmented quick filters + tile multi-select (checkbox on hover) revealing a bulk-action bar.  WHY — for scanning a large set fast (workspaces, integrations, repos): tiny tiles maximize how many you see at once, and hover-select keeps them clean until you act on several.

Composition 5
Spotlight + grid

PULLS — a featured hero card spanning full width on top, then a normal card grid below + (second panel) the empty / first-run state: a centered message, primary “Add” CTA, and a hint line.  WHY — editorial & home surfaces lead with one promoted item to set context, then the grid carries the rest; the empty state turns a blank first run into an obvious next step.

Skins & how to apply them

A skin is nothing more than a small set of color + font tokens. Pick ONE skin per product and apply it across whichever compositions you choose — the composition is the skeleton, the skin is the paint. Every composition above is drawn in the Neutral skin; swap the token row and the same skeletons render coherently in any of the others.
