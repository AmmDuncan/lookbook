Composed Recipe · Calendar / scheduling · one skin, five compositions

Same skin. Five ways to show time.

One week of meetings — the team's June schedule — composed five ways. Every frame wears the same neutral skin: off-white page, white surfaces with hairline borders, slightly-rounded corners, one restrained blue accent, system sans with tabular figures. What changes is purely composition and interaction. The skin is the paint, the composition is the skeleton.

Composition 1
Month grid

PULLS — a month calendar grid with event chips in day cells + a header (prev / next / Today + Month / Week / Day view switcher).  WHY — the default overview: a month at a glance shows density and free days, while the view switcher hands off to finer-grained views when needed.

Apply
Skins & how to apply them

A skin is the full set of visual tokens — color, type, radius, elevation, and density — applied uniformly. Pick ONE skin per product and apply it across whichever compositions you choose: the composition is the skeleton, the skin is the paint, so any composition above renders coherently in any skin. Every frame on this page uses the Neutral column.
