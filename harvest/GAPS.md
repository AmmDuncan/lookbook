# GAPS — where the variation layer didn't cover the surface

The shopping list for `harvest/specimens/`.

`FLOW.md` step 5 says the specimens are the **first place to look** for layout and
composition, not the only allowed source. When they don't fit and you take the
composition from a real product or generate an original, **add a line here.** That is the
whole cost of the escape hatch, and it is what turns "not mature enough yet" into a
finite list of things to harvest instead of a permanent excuse.

Log the gap when the specimens **didn't cover the surface**. Don't log a preference — if a
specimen fit and you chose otherwise, that is a taste call, not a gap.

## Format

One row per gap. Keep `Nearest specimen` honest: naming the closest thing and why it
missed is the part that tells a harvest what to build.

| Date | Surface / archetype | What I needed | Nearest specimen | Why it missed | Went with |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

_No gaps logged yet — this file was added 2026-08-18, at the same time as the rule that
feeds it. An empty table here means the rule is new, not that the coverage is complete._

## Reading it

- **A surface appearing 2+ times** is a harvest target: build the variation sheet.
- **"Nearest specimen" repeatedly naming the same file** means that sheet is close but
  under-varied — extend it rather than starting a new one.
- **"Went with: original" dominating** means the layer is behind on that whole family.

When a gap is closed by a new specimen, strike the row and name the file that closed it.
