Composed Recipe · Checkout / multi-step wizard · one skin, five compositions

Same skin. Five ways to check out.

One purchase — two items, shipping, payment, review — composed five ways. Every frame wears the same neutral skin: off-white page, white surfaces with hairline borders, slightly-rounded corners, one restrained blue accent, system sans with tabular figures. Fields use placeholder labels only — no real payment data. What changes is purely composition and interaction.

Composition 1
Stepper + summary rail

PULLS — a horizontal stepper (Cart → Shipping → Payment → Review) + the current step panel on the left + a sticky order-summary rail on the right (line items, subtotal, tax, total).  WHY — the conversion-tested default: explicit steps set expectations and the ever-present total reassures buyers there are no surprises before they pay.

Skins & how to apply them

A skin is the full set of visual tokens — color, type, radius, elevation, and density — applied uniformly. Pick ONE skin per product and apply it across whichever compositions you choose: the composition is the skeleton, the skin is the paint, so any composition above renders coherently in any skin. Every frame on this page uses the Neutral column.
