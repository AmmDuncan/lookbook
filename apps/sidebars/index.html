A sidebar is six dials, not one look

The left rail is the spine of nearly every product app — and the most defaulted surface in the system. The depth rubric's running joke is true: a dark sidebar is one option, not the answer. Here are the other eleven. Each is the same six dials set differently — on one token contract, no new colour, no one-off parts. Earned from real shipped rails: Linear, Stripe, Slack, Notion, Supabase, Sentry, Superhuman, the macOS Finder, Grafana.

The six dialsevery rail below resolves to these — "not dynamic enough" almost always means dial 6 is set to static on a shell that needed motion

1 · Width

icon-rail 56px · standard 220–260 · wide 280–320 · dual · collapsible

2 · Surface

dark (value-anchor) · light (seam-anchor) · tinted · glass · inset card

3 · Active carrier

filled pill · left bar · tinted-bg + accent text · subtle fill · dot. Exactly one — never all three.

4 · Grouping

flat · labelled sections · collapsible tree · pinned + sections

5 · The sandwich

top: switcher / ⌘K · bottom: account / upgrade / status

6 · Dynamism

static · collapse · hover-peek · contextual-secondary · switch · tree-expand

Why it works

The rail is a translucent material that reads as OS-integrated. The discipline: a semi-opaque scrim floor under the blur so labels never drop below 4.5:1 when a bright backdrop scrolls behind — or it fails the contrast gate instantly.

Reach for it when

A genuinely native-feeling desktop app — and nowhere else. It's the one surface that's a liability reached for reflexively and a delight when the platform earns it.

Why it works

Every dial set to its safest value — filled-pill active (unmistakable), the sandwich (switcher top / account bottom), a pinned trial card above the account (the real, repeated B2B pattern). This is the baseline; the eleven above are deviations from it.

Reach for it when

You don't have a reason to pick another. Correct and unremarkable beats clever and wrong — then borrow one move from 01–11 to make it yours.

The single invariant across all twelve: six dials + the sandwich, one accent, one active-carrier, a real seam to the content well. They differ by how the dials are set and which dynamism is layered on — never by inventing colour, spacing, or a one-off part. That's converge-on-vocabulary / diverge-on-composition, applied to the most-defaulted surface in the system.
