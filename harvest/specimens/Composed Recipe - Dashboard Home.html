Composed Recipe · Dashboard home / landing · one skin, five compositions

Same skin. Five ways to say “welcome back.”

The first screen after login, composed five ways. Every frame wears the same neutral skin — off-white page, white cards with hairline borders, one restrained blue accent, system sans with tabular figures. What changes is purely composition and interaction: how the home greets you, what it leads with, how widgets are arranged. The skin is the paint, the composition is the skeleton.

Composition 1
Greeting + widget grid

PULLS — personalized greeting header + a row of quick-action buttons, then a uniform grid of widget cards (KPI tile, small line chart, recent-activity list, tasks-due list).  WHY — the friendly default home: a greeting orients you, quick actions cover the top jobs, and an even widget grid gives every signal equal, scannable weight.

Composition 2
Bento grid

PULLS — varied-size bento tiles (some span 2 columns or 2 rows) mixing one big metric, a chart tile, a list tile, and a CTA tile — asymmetric but aligned to a grid.  WHY — when widgets have unequal importance: size becomes hierarchy, so the hero metric and chart dominate while smaller tiles fill in, all locked to one tidy grid.

Composition 3
Focus / today

PULLS — a prominent left “Today” column (agenda & tasks with checkboxes) + a secondary right column of smaller widgets (a stat, a mini chart, shortcuts).  WHY — a do-first home: it answers “what needs me today” before any metrics, with the supporting numbers kept deliberately secondary on the right.

Composition 4
Feed-centric

PULLS — sidebar nav + a central activity/feed column (what happened) + a right rail of compact widgets (quick stats, upcoming, suggestions).  WHY — for collaborative or fast-moving products: the feed is the home, telling you what changed since you left, while the rail keeps a few numbers and next steps within reach.

Composition 5
Onboarding home

PULLS — a setup-checklist card (steps with progress) as the hero + placeholder/empty widget cards with subtle prompts to connect data + a primary CTA. The first-run / empty state.  WHY — a brand-new account has no data to show, so the home's job flips to activation: one obvious checklist drives setup while empty widgets preview the payoff.

Skins & how to apply them

A skin is nothing more than a small set of color + font tokens. Pick ONE skin per product and apply it across whichever compositions you choose — the composition is the skeleton, the skin is the paint. Every composition above is drawn in the Neutral skin; swap the token row and the same skeletons render coherently in any of the others.
