Composed Recipe · Activity feed / notifications · one skin, five compositions

Same skin. Five ways to say “here's what happened.”

One stream of events — comments, mentions, approvals, uploads across the workspace — composed five ways. Every frame wears the same neutral skin: off-white page, white surfaces with hairline borders, slightly-rounded corners, one restrained blue accent, system sans with tabular figures. What changes is purely composition and interaction. The skin is the paint, the composition is the skeleton.

Composition 1
Grouped timeline

PULLS — a vertical timeline grouped by date (Today / Yesterday / Earlier) with rows of actor avatar + action text + target + relative time, joined by a connecting rail line.  WHY — for a history you read top-to-bottom: date groups give rhythm and the rail makes the sequence feel continuous rather than a flat list.

Notifications center

PULLS — a list of notifications with read/unread states (unread dot + tint) + filter tabs (All / Mentions / Following) + a “Mark all read” action.  WHY — the inbox model for alerts: unread state plus a one-click clear turns a noisy stream into a queue you can actually get to zero.

Apply
Skins & how to apply them

A skin is the full set of visual tokens — color, type, radius, elevation, and density — applied uniformly. Pick ONE skin per product and apply it across whichever compositions you choose: the composition is the skeleton, the skin is the paint, so any composition above renders coherently in any skin. Every frame on this page uses the Neutral column.
